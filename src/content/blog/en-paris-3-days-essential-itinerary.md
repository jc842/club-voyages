# Paris in 3 Days: The Perfect Itinerary for an Unforgettable Escape

With its timeless limestone facades, world-class gastronomy, and legendary art collections, Paris remains the quintessential European city break. Here is your curated 3-day roadmap to experiencing the City of Light with elegance and ease.

---

## Day 1: Historic Heart & Royal Paris

- **Morning**: Start at the **Louvre Museum**, entering through the Cour Carrée to admire the Mona Lisa, Winged Victory, and Venus de Milo before the largest crowds arrive.
- **Midday**: Walk through the **Tuileries Gardens** to Place de la Concorde, stopping for a pastry at a traditional salon de thé.
- **Afternoon**: Stroll along the **Île de la Cité**, marvel at the newly restored spires of **Notre-Dame Cathedral**, and step inside the stained-glass wonder of **Sainte-Chapelle**.
- **Sunset**: Take a sunset cruise on the Seine River aboard a private Riva boat or classic vedette.

## Day 2: Left Bank Elegance & The Eiffel Tower

- **Morning**: Wander through **Saint-Germain-des-Prés**, lingering with café au lait at Café de Flore or Les Deux Magots.
- **Midday**: Relax in the serene gardens of **Jardin du Luxembourg** among Medici fountains and chestnut trees.
- **Afternoon**: Visit the **Musée d'Orsay** to admire the greatest Impressionist masterpieces by Monet, Renoir, and Van Gogh.
- **Evening**: Head to the Trocadéro for panoramic views of the **Eiffel Tower** as it sparkles on the hour.

## Day 3: Bohemian Montmartre & The Marais

- **Morning**: Climb the winding alleys of **Montmartre** to the Sacré-Cœur basilica, discovering hidden vineyards and artist ateliers along Place du Tertre.
- **Afternoon**: Cross over to **Le Marais**, visiting Place des Vosges, contemporary art galleries, and historic private mansions (Hôtels Particuliers).
- **Dinner**: Reserve a table at a classic neo-bistro serving seasonal French culinary craftsmanship with natural wines.

---

## Club Voyages Travel Advice
- **Museum Pass**: Pre-book timed-entry slots for both the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay to skip general admission queues.
- **Airport Transfer**: RER B or official taxis (fixed fare €56-€65 from Charles de Gaulle Airport).
