# Dubrovnik: Exploring the Pearl of the Adriatic in 3 Days

With its fortified medieval walls surrounded by crystalline turquoise waters, Dubrovnik is one of the Mediterranean's most spectacular coastal treasures.

---

## 3-Day Highlights
- **The Ancient City Walls**: Walk the full 2-kilometer circuit atop the ramparts for panoramic sea views.
- **The Stradun**: Stroll the smooth limestone pedestrian thoroughfare connecting the Pile and Ploče gates.
- **Lokrum Island**: A 15-minute ferry brings you to botanical gardens and peacocks.
- **Mount Srđ Sunset**: Ride the cable car for sunset drinks overlooking the fortified citadel.
