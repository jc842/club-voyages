# Capri: The Ultimate Island Guide to Villages, Boat Charters & Faraglioni

Rising dramatically from the Tyrrhenian Sea, the island of Capri has captivated roman emperors, artists, and international jet-setters for centuries.

---

## Essential Island Experiences
- **The Faraglioni Rocks**: Charter a traditional wooden *gozzo* boat to sail through the natural stone arch.
- **Anacapri & Mount Solaro**: Ride the chairlift to the highest peak on the island for 360-degree views over the Bay of Naples and Mount Vesuvius.
- **Piazzetta di Capri**: Sip an espresso or Limoncello while watching glamour unfold.
