# Vienna: 25 Imperial Highlights, Palaces and Coffeehouse Culture

Vienna exudes imperial splendor and refined living. Former home of Mozart, Beethoven, and the Habsburg dynasty, Austria's capital pairs majestic architecture with cozy heritage coffeehouses.

---

## Highlights of the Imperial Ringstraße
- **Schönbrunn Palace**: The summer residence of the Habsburgs with its sprawling baroque gardens and Gloriette.
- **The Hofburg**: The winter imperial palace, Imperial Apartments, and the Spanish Riding School.
- **Belvedere Palace**: Home to Gustav Klimt's shimmering masterpiece *The Kiss*.
- **St. Stephen's Cathedral (Stephansdom)**: The gothic heart of Vienna.

## Coffeehouse Culture & Gastronomy
No visit is complete without lingering in a traditional Viennese café:
- Café Central, Café Sacher (for original Sachertorte), and Café Sperl.
- Enjoy a melange coffee alongside crisp Wiener Schnitzel and Apfelstrudel.
