# Chiang Mai Travel Guide: Temples, Mountain Retreats & Old City Charms

Nestled among the mist-covered mountains of Northern Thailand, Chiang Mai offers a peaceful cultural counterpoint to bustling Bangkok.

---

## Highlights
- **Wat Phra That Doi Suthep**: Perched atop the mountain with sweeping views over the city.
- **The Old City Moat & Temples**: Wat Chedi Luang and Wat Phra Singh.
- **Night Bazaar & Sunday Walking Street**: Northern Thai handicrafts, silk, and aromatic khao soi curry noodles.
