# New York in 5 Days: The Ultimate Itinerary for First-Time Visitors

The electric pulse of Manhattan, iconic skyline silhouettes at sunset, and an unstoppable culinary scene make New York City one of the world's most exhilarating destinations. For a first visit, five days strikes the perfect balance between must-see highlights and authentic neighborhood discoveries.

---

## Day 1: Midtown Manhattan Icons & High Line

Begin your journey right at the beating heart of the city:
- **Morning**: Stroll through **Times Square** before the midday rush, then head to **Rockefeller Center** and the neo-gothic grandeur of **St. Patrick's Cathedral**.
- **Afternoon**: Walk west along the **High Line**, an elevated park crafted on historic freight rail lines, leading directly into the architectural spectacle of **Hudson Yards** and The Vessel.
- **Evening**: Ascend the **Edge** or **Top of the Rock** for golden hour panoramas over the Empire State Building and Central Park.

## Day 2: Lower Manhattan, Financial District & Brooklyn Bridge

- **Morning**: Pay your respects at the **9/11 Memorial & Museum**, then admire the soaring vaulted ceiling of the **Oculus** and Wall Street's Trinity Church.
- **Afternoon**: Embark on the Staten Island Ferry or a private harbor cruise past the **Statue of Liberty** and Ellis Island.
- **Sunset Walk**: Traverse the historic pedestrian walkway of the **Brooklyn Bridge** toward **DUMBO** for dinner with sweeping skyline views.

## Day 3: Culture & Greenery — Central Park & Upper East Side

- **Morning**: Explore the vast pathways of **Central Park** — Bow Bridge, Bethesda Terrace, and the Ramble.
- **Afternoon**: Delve into the treasures of the **Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met)** or the modern masterpieces at the **Guggenheim**.
- **Evening**: Enjoy an intimate jazz club experience in Greenwich Village or catch a Broadway performance.

## Day 4: Soho, Greenwich Village & Chelsea

Discover the chic cobblestone streets, designer boutiques, and bohemian legacy of downtown Manhattan:
- **Morning**: Coffee and shopping along Broadway, Prince Street, and Mercer Street in **Soho**.
- **Afternoon**: Discover Washington Square Park and the historic brownstones of **West Village**.
- **Dinner**: Indulge in Italian trattorias in Greenwich Village or artisan bites at Chelsea Market.

## Day 5: Brooklyn Arts & Williamsburg

- **Morning**: Explore the vibrant street art, independent bookshops, and vintage boutiques of **Williamsburg**.
- **Afternoon**: Walk through Domino Park along the East River overlooking Midtown.
- **Farewell Dinner**: Sip cocktails at a rooftop lounge in Brooklyn facing the illuminated Manhattan skyline.
