# Miami Neighborhood Guide: South Beach, Brickell, Design District & Wynwood

Basking in year-round sunshine, Miami is an international hub of art, fashion, gastronomy, and seaside glamour.

---

## 1. South Beach & Art Deco District
Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue feature the world's largest collection of Art Deco architecture. Perfect for beachfront luxury and chic lounges.

## 2. Brickell: The Manhattan of the South
Sleek glass skyscrapers, rooftop bars overlooking Biscayne Bay, and high-end dining at Brickell City Centre.

## 3. Design District & Wynwood
- **Miami Design District**: Haute couture flagship stores, public sculpture installations, and Michelin-starred restaurants.
- **Wynwood Walls**: The epicenter of global street art and craft breweries.
