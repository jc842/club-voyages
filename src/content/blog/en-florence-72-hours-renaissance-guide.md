# Florence in 72 Hours: Masterpieces of the Italian Renaissance & Tuscan Living

Cradle of the Renaissance, Florence is an open-air museum where Botticelli, Leonardo da Vinci, and Michelangelo shaped Western art.

---

## Day 1: The Duomo & Historic Core
- **Morning**: Stand beneath **Brunelleschi's magnificent Dome** at the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore and climb Giotto's Campanile.
- **Afternoon**: Walk to the **Piazza della Signoria**, admiring the outdoor sculpture museum at the Loggia dei Lanzi.
- **Sunset**: Cross the iconic **Ponte Vecchio** jewelry bridge as dusk settles over the Arno River.

## Day 2: The Uffizi & Michelangelo's David
- **Morning**: Explore the **Uffizi Gallery**, viewing Botticelli's *The Birth of Venus* and masterpieces by Caravaggio and Raphael.
- **Afternoon**: Stand before the awe-inspiring original sculpture of **David** at the **Galleria dell'Accademia**.
- **Dinner**: Savor a traditional *Bistecca alla Fiorentina* paired with a bold Chianti Classico.

## Day 3: Oltrarno Artisans & Piazzale Michelangelo
- **Morning**: Cross into the bohemian **Oltrarno** district, exploring leather craftsmen, antique paper marblers, and the Pitti Palace.
- **Afternoon**: Stroll through the terraced Boboli Gardens.
- **Sunset**: Ascend to **Piazzale Michelangelo** for the most famous postcard panorama over the red roofs and dome of Florence.
