# Rome in 72 Hours: The Eternal City Experience

Nowhere else on Earth blends millennia of living history with effortless modern glamour like Rome. In 72 hours, you can trace the steps of emperors, stand beneath Michelangelo's frescoes, and savor legendary pasta in cobblestone piazzas.

---

## Day 1: Imperial Rome & The Colosseum

- **Morning**: Enter the **Colosseum** with arena floor access, then explore the ruins of the **Roman Forum** and Palatine Hill where emperors built their grand palaces.
- **Afternoon**: Walk to the **Capitoline Hill** designed by Michelangelo and take in the view over the ancient city.
- **Evening**: Throw a coin into the **Trevi Fountain** under evening illuminations, then climb the **Spanish Steps** for a rooftop aperitivo.

## Day 2: The Vatican & Trastevere

- **Morning**: Visit **St. Peter's Basilica** and the **Vatican Museums**, marveling at the Sistine Chapel ceiling.
- **Afternoon**: Walk along the Tiber River past Castel Sant'Angelo toward the bohemian quarter of **Trastevere**.
- **Dinner**: Dine in an authentic osteria savoring true Roman classics: *Cacio e Pepe*, *Carbonara*, and crispy *Carciofi alla Giudia*.

## Day 3: Baroque Splendor & The Pantheon

- **Morning**: Stand inside the **Pantheon**, admiring the 2,000-year-old unreinforced concrete dome and its open oculus.
- **Midday**: Enjoy espresso at Sant'Eustachio Il Caffè and explore **Piazza Navona** with Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers.
- **Afternoon**: Visit Campo de' Fiori market and the lush gardens of **Villa Borghese**.
