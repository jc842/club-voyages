# Canada in 14 Days: Grand Roadtrip from Rocky Mountains to Vibrant Cities

Vast wilderness, turquoise glacial lakes, and cosmopolitan cities: Canada is the ultimate road trip destination for nature lovers seeking grand panoramas.

---

## Week 1: The Majestic Canadian Rockies
- **Banff National Park**: Turquoise brilliance of **Lake Louise** and **Moraine Lake**, surrounded by snow-capped peaks.
- **Icefields Parkway**: Rated one of the world's most scenic drives, passing ancient glaciers and cascading waterfalls.
- **Jasper National Park**: Wildlife sightings including elk, bighorn sheep, and bears.

## Week 2: West Coast Coastal Beauty — Vancouver & Island
- **Vancouver**: Stanley Park seawall, Gastown steam clock, and world-class Pacific seafood.
- **Vancouver Island**: Whale watching in Tofino and the picturesque British charm of Victoria.
