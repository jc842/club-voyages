# French Caribbean Luxury: Elite Villas, Turquoise Lagoons & Gourmet Living

The French West Indies — Guadeloupe and Martinique — offer a refined blend of Caribbean warmth and French culinary excellence (*art de vivre*).

---

## Prestige Villa Rentals in Saint-François & Sainte-Anne
For travelers seeking privacy and comfort, private luxury villas offer:
- **Infinity Pools** facing the turquoise Atlantic Ocean.
- Direct private access to quiet beaches.
- In-villa private chefs preparing fresh lobster, grilled mahi-mahi, and tropical fruit creations.

## Marine Excursions & Yacht Charters
- **Petite Terre Nature Reserve**: Sail by catamaran to swim alongside sea turtles, rays, and harmless lemon sharks in a protected coral lagoon.
- **Les Saintes Bay**: Ranked among the most beautiful bays in the world, with Fort Napoléon and fine Creole dining.
