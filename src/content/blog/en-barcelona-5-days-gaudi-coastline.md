# Barcelona in 5 Days: Gaudí Architecture, Catalan Flavors & Coastal Allure

Sun-drenched Mediterranean shores meet breathtaking Modernist architecture in the capital of Catalonia. Five days allows you to discover Gaudí's masterpieces without rushing, whilst soaking up beach vibes and culinary wizardry.

---

## Day 1: Modernisme & Passeig de Gràcia
- **Morning**: Marvel at Gaudí's **Casa Batlló** and the undulating stone facade of **La Pedrera (Casa Milà)**.
- **Afternoon**: Stroll down the tree-lined boulevard of **Passeig de Gràcia** into Plaça de Catalunya.
- **Evening**: Tapas crawl around the chic Eixample district with jamón ibérico, pan con tomate, and local cava.

## Day 2: Sagrada Família & Park Güell
- **Morning**: Step into the forest of stone and stained glass inside the **Sagrada Família** — reserve the tower ascent for sweeping city views.
- **Afternoon**: Head up to **Park Güell** to admire the vibrant mosaic salamander and panoramic Mediterranean vistas.

## Day 3: Gothic Quarter, El Born & The Sea
- **Morning**: Lose yourself in the medieval maze of the **Barri Gòtic**, the Cathedral of Barcelona, and Plaça del Rei.
- **Afternoon**: Browse artisanal boutiques in **El Born**, visit the **Picasso Museum**, and walk along the **Barceloneta** beachfront.
- **Sunset**: Fresh seafood paella overlooking the marina at Port Olímpic.

## Day 4: Montjuïc & Contemporary Culture
- **Morning**: Ride the cable car to **Montjuïc Castle** and visit the Fundació Joan Miró.
- **Afternoon**: Explore the **Boqueria Market** on Las Ramblas for fresh fruit juices and Iberian charcuterie.

## Day 5: Day Trip to Montserrat or Sitges
- Take a scenic 1-hour train ride to the jagged peaks and sacred abbey of **Montserrat**, or unwind on the golden beaches of coastal **Sitges**.
