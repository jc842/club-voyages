# London in 72 Hours: Royal Heritage, West End & Modern Luxury

London blends historic pageantry with cutting-edge creativity. In three days, discover the crown jewels, world-renowned theatre, serene royal parks, and Michelin-starred dining.

---

## Day 1: Royal Westminster & The Thames
- **Morning**: Witness Big Ben, the Houses of Parliament, and the royal history of **Westminster Abbey**.
- **Afternoon**: Walk through St James's Park to **Buckingham Palace**, then stroll up the Mall to Trafalgar Square and the **National Gallery**.
- **Evening**: Dine in **Covent Garden** and attend an acclaimed theatrical performance in the **West End**.

## Day 2: The City of London, Tower & South Bank
- **Morning**: Uncover 1,000 years of royal secrets at the **Tower of London** and cross **Tower Bridge**.
- **Afternoon**: Explore **Tate Modern** on the South Bank, browse gourmet delicacies at **Borough Market**, and take a flight on the **London Eye**.
- **Evening**: Reserve a skyline cocktail at The Shard or Sky Garden.

## Day 3: Mayfair, Knightsbridge & Kensington
- **Morning**: Stroll along luxury boutiques on Bond Street and Mount Street in **Mayfair**.
- **Afternoon**: Discover Harrods in **Knightsbridge** and the extraordinary collections at the **V&A Museum**.
- **Afternoon Tea**: Experience traditional High Tea at Claridge's or The Ritz.
