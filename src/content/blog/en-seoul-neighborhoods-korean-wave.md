# Seoul City Guide: Best Neighborhoods, Tradition & Modern K-Wave

Hyper-modern skyscrapers stand alongside 600-year-old royal dynasties in Seoul, one of the most dynamic cities in the world.

---

## Neighborhood Breakdown
- **Bukchon Hanok Village & Insadong**: Traditional wooden Korean architecture, tea houses, and calligraphy shops.
- **Myeongdong**: Street food paradise (tteokbokki, hotteok, dumplings) and cosmetics boutiques.
- **Gangnam**: Apgujeong and Cheongdam-dong for high luxury, K-pop culture, and futuristic architecture.
- **Hongdae & Yeonnam-dong**: Youthful energy, indie cafes, busking, and vibrant nightlife.
